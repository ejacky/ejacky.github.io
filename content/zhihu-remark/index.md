# Privacy Policy

Refute Assistant for Zhihu does not collect personally identifiable information.

The extension processes selected webpage content only for generating AI-assisted responses.

User content may be sent to third-party AI providers (such as OpenAI-compatible APIs) for response generation.

No user data is sold, permanently stored, or shared for advertising purposes.

## Data Usage

The extension may access:

- Selected text content
- Current webpage content
- User input submitted to AI services

These data are used solely for:

- Generating AI-assisted rebuttals
- Improving response relevance during the current session

## Data Storage

This extension does not permanently store user content on external servers.

Temporary processing may occur when communicating with AI APIs.

## Third-Party Services

The extension may communicate with third-party AI providers, such as:

- OpenAI
- DeepSeek
- Google Gemini

Data sent to these services is processed according to their respective privacy policies.
